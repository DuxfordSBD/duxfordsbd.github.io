What a fantastic day it was at the 2026 Duxford Soap Box Derby! Congratulations to Tom Sale and Alex Snow of "Trojan Horse Power", who took the adults race and the Phill Hill Trophy with a time of 9 minutes and 7 seconds. A personal best for the pair.

Congratulations are also in order to the winners of the children's races:

* 4-7 age group was won by team "Knight Bus", Rafferty Hodge and Alfie Hodge
* 8-11 age group was won by team "Twin Turbo", Ernest Tautz and Lilian Tautz
* 12-15 age group was won by team "Almost Fast and Not So Furious", Edward Lober and Tom Varley

And a nod to "Lost Property" (David Brown and Ian Brown), who picked up the Wooden Spoon this year.
