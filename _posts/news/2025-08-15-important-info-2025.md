## Important News

**All races to start and end at The Plough**

Due to the recent closure of the John Barleycorn, the organising committee has taken drastic action and moved our entire event to the Plough. This includes scrutineering, race registration and all races. We are grateful to Mario, Tracey and the Plough for stepping in and agreeing to host the entire event. 

## Information on the day itself

You can find information on the [order of events for this year's derby]({% link pages/about/programme-of-events-2025.md %}) including a family fun day and silent disco all [organised by The Lodge, Duxford](https://thelodgeduxford.co.uk/).

## Impact on the event

All of our race routes have been adapted so that each one starts at the Plough, departing down St Peter’s St and, eventually, returning back up St Peter’s St to the finish line. All [routes are available online]({% link participating/route-map.md %}) and we are sure they will be as enjoyable as our traditional routes.

## Road Closures
Relevant roads will be officially closed from 11.30am until 2.30pm. If you live on the race routes please park in a way that offers the carts a clear run, especially if you usually park on St Peter’s St. The competitors are not winning any prizes for steering and we want to ensure a safe uneventful race for everyone.
