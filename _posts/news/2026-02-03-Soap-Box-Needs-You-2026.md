We have a very important announcement. Likely you will have seen our posters around Duxford village asking for support with our 2026 event. Without volunteers to help run the Soap Box derby this year's event might not go ahead. There are a huge number of oppertunities including:

- Road closure logistics
- Event management
- Media liason
- Meeting minutes & administration
- Treasurer
- Prize sourcing for our auction and raffle
- Website management
- Social media management
- Project management

If this sounds like something you would be interested in you can come along to our next meeting on the 11th February 6pm at the John Barleycorn, Duxford. No obligation to join but see what the derby is all about.
