After another fantastic year and an unforgettable Duxford Soap Box Derby 2025, we are delighted to announce this year’s final total.

Thanks to the incredible generosity of our racers, supporters, volunteers and sponsors, we raised **£6,706** in aid of **Cancer Research UK**. This brings our overall total raised since the event began to an amazing **£100,249**, passing the £100k milestone.

A huge thank you goes out to all of our supporters, volunteers, prize donors and our wonderful venue The Plough especially Mario, Tracey and their team's tireless efforts who stepped in at the last minute to support the derby from start to finish. We also thank Gen and everyone at Four, Duxford who were such good hosts and brought the carnival atmosphere to our event. The Duxford Soap Box Derby simply wouldn’t be possible without your continued support.

You can find out more about everyone who helped make this year such a success on our [supporters thanks page]({% link pages/about/with-thanks-to.md %}).

Thank you once again to everyone who helped make Duxford Soap Box Derby 2025 such a special and meaningful event.
