The Duxford Soap Box Derby will take place on the 31st August 2025 in [Duxford, Cambridgeshire][location].

### Sunday 24th August
[Family fun day hosted by The Lodge, Duxford](https://www.facebook.com/thelodgeduxford/posts/pfbid0244kXp29R27Q4Yb3WcTmcrweJq6YRRCHcGqo7bLXLNiZqQnFanzkUYS5nH4GRo6c4l)
* 12pm to 7pm
* Ickleton Rd, Duxford, Cambridge CB22 4RT
* BBQ, cocktails and craft Beer
* Assault course
* Bouncy castle
* Football darts
* Face painting
* Much much more

### Friday 29th August
[Silent Disco & Street Food hosted by The Lodge, Duxford](https://www.facebook.com/thelodgeduxford/posts/pfbid0244kXp29R27Q4Yb3WcTmcrweJq6YRRCHcGqo7bLXLNiZqQnFanzkUYS5nH4GRo6c4l)
* 7–10:30PM
* Book Now: <hello@thelodgeduxford.com> / 01223 755677
* Ickleton Rd, Duxford, Cambridge CB22 4RT
* All donations go directly to supporting the Soap Box Derby

### Sunday 31st August

**All events will happen at the Plough, Duxford, 57-59 St Peter's St, Duxford, Cambridge CB22 4RP**

* __10:00 am - 11:20 am__
* Registration of vehicles, scrutineering and racers briefing
* Please attend the farmyard across from the Plough

**Road closures start at 11:30**

### Parade of Carts
* __11:40 am__
Starting at registration, departing down towards the war memorial and finishing at the Plough

### Sunday 1st September Duxford Soap Box Derby Races
[The Plough, Duxford](https://www.facebook.com/theploughatduxford/), St Peter's St

* __12:00 Noon Children’s Race__
    * Race one: 4-7 and 8-11 year olds
    * Race two: 12-15 year olds
    * Children's race winner presentations
* __1:30pm Adult’s Race__
    * The Phill Hill Cup

### … And Finish
* __2pm onwards__
    * Presentation of awards
    * Live music
    * Face painting
    * Auction and raffle draw
    * Hog roast, burgers and ice cream on sale

**Road closures end at 14:30**
