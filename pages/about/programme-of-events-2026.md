The Duxford Soap Box Derby will take place on the 6th September 2026 in [Duxford, Cambridgeshire][location].

The full programme of events — pre-derby pub nights, registration and scrutineering times, road closure times, and the parade route — is still being finalised and will be published here closer to the date. Please check back, or watch our [news page]({{ "/news" | absolute_url }}) and social media for updates.

### Duxford Soap Box Derby Races

Race day timings are expected to be approximately the same as in 2024 and 2025:

* **11:30am** road closures start
* __approx. 12:00 Noon Children’s Race__
    * Race one: 4-7 and 8-11 year olds
    * Race two: 12-15 year olds
* __approx. 1:30pm Adult’s Race__
    * The Phill Hill Cup
* **2:30pm** roads re-open
